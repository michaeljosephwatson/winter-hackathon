Venture Through the Forest

Grow your empathy along the way ...

A Gentle Game

Designed to be felt, not beaten.

Play Without Pressure

No timers or scores, play at your own pace.

Rooted in Nature

Inspired by real forests and how they are sustained.

Accessibility Options:

You sprout. Sprouted from the ground you are a sapling. A sapling you are not meant to be. You know, you are meant to grow.

Sapling, you spy a new sprout, and another, and another, and soon a whole field of fresh sprouts you spy. You deliberate how tall you could grow with other sprouts clambering for sunlight.

Sapling, do you:

You grow in height to be the tallest tree in the world. Your growth journey ends here.
You have accepted the new sprouts, you do not grow to be the tallest tree. You have grown to become a forest.

Forest of trees, you gaze on your bounty of bark and leaves of green and notice a bug. Two bugs. Three bugs. A whole army of bugs, grubs and wrigglers.

You deliberate how beautiful green you could grow with an army of bugs swarming your bark and swindling your leaves.

Forest of trees, do you:

You grow in green to be the greenest forest in the world. Your growth journey ends here.
Forest of Trees and Wriggling feet, you feel the strength and splendor of your trunks and branches, until…

Chop Chop. Snap Snap, Crunch Crunch, Creak, Crash.

You feel a section of your trunks tumble to the ground and spot the crafty culprits. Beavers.

Your new sprouts grew your forest but shrunk your height and attracted bugs which grew your soil but shrunk your beautiful green, and attracted a frenzy of fauna shrinking your forest.

You deliberate how much more you will shrink if you accept these fauna. Or how much more will you grow.

Do you:

You do not shrink in size but remain as you are. Your growth journey ends here.
You have accepted the fauna, and the bugs, grubs and wrigglers, and the new sprouts. You have grown to become a fire and drought resistant, soil-rich forest.

You have grown your compassion. Your forest of trees and wriggling feet and fauna has grown you to have the strongest and most beautiful heart. Your growth journey does not end here.
